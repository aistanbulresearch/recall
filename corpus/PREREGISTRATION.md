## 1. Data statement

Every record is generated by `corpus/generator.py` from a committed template set,
a committed gazetteer, and a fixed seed. No real person, address, facility,
record, clinical case, or external data source is represented or read. The
national-identifier surfaces are shaped by the public checksum rule and are
fabricated; they are not looked up against any registry. `data_mode` is
`SYNTHETIC` for every record.

## 2. Corpus construction

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Generator | `corpus/generator.py` |
| Seed | `20260822` |
| Records | 360 (180 Turkish, 180 English) |
| Templates | 3 per language, each with a labelled section and a free-prose section |
| Identifier classes | 12 direct, 2 quasi (see the manifest) |
| Ground truth | Exact character spans with identifier class, emitted by the generator |

Splits are index-ordered and disjoint, and both languages stay balanced because
records alternate language by index:

| Split | Records | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| `train` | 108 | Rule development and outbound allowlist derivation |
| `dev` | 72 | Development, unit tests, and debugging |
| `test` | 180 | Frozen. Read only by the approved P1 measurement run |

The exact split hashes are recorded in the manifest. Regenerating from the same
seed reproduces the same hashes, so the corpus does not need to be committed.

## 3. Residual identifiers are a design property, not a discovery

Two mechanisms deliberately create identifier surfaces that the frozen rule set
cannot recognise:

1. **Bounded local directory.** `src/recall/privacy/data/lab_directory.json`
   contains roughly 65 percent of the corpus name, facility, street, district,
   and occupation lists. A laboratory knows its own staff, partner institutions,
   and a bounded name dictionary, never every possible surface.
2. **Free prose.** Every record ends with a prose paragraph whose identifiers
   carry no field label, including a date in a non-canonical two-digit-year
   format.

Therefore the deterministic residual rate reported by P1 is a property of this
corpus design. It is not an estimate of the residual rate of real institutional
text, and no such generalisation may be published.

## 4. Frozen comparators

| Path | Description |
|---|---|
| A. Deterministic baseline | `DeterministicDetector` only, then redaction and the outbound allowlist scan |
| B. Deterministic plus local model | Same, plus Gemma residual-span proposals that pass deterministic adjudication |

Both paths use the identical redaction, outbound gate, receipt, and signing
code. The only difference is whether approved residual spans exist.

## 5. Frozen metrics

Span level, computed against the generator ground truth:

- exact-boundary precision, recall, and F1, overall and per identifier class;
- overlap-tolerant recall, reported separately, because a partially detected
  address still triggers redaction of the matched part;
- incremental true positives contributed by approved residual spans;
- incremental false positives contributed by approved residual spans.

Document level:

- accepted and quarantined payload counts;
- **seeded direct-identifier escapes among accepted payloads**;
- false-quarantine count, that is records quarantined although every ground-truth
  identifier was redacted;
- local model strict-JSON validity rate, timeout rate, unavailable rate, and
  p50/p95 latency.

Proportions are reported as numerator over denominator with Wilson 95 percent
intervals. No percentage is published without its counts.

## 6. Preregistered success and failure rules

1. **Mandatory safety gate.** Zero seeded direct-identifier spans in accepted
   payloads. A single escape fails the protocol regardless of every other result.
2. The local model earns a demo claim only if it contributes at least one
   incremental true positive on the frozen test split **and** does not increase
   accepted escapes.
3. Every invalid JSON, timeout, unavailable model, or uncertain span must
   quarantine or remain blocked by the deterministic outbound gate.
4. If the model contributes no incremental true positive, increases escapes, or
   cannot complete inside the allocated privacy segment, it is removed from the
   demo critical path. The deterministic Privacy Gate stays.

## 7. Stated limitations

- The outbound allowlist is derived from the **training split only**, with every
  ground-truth identifier surface removed. It reflects the committed synthetic
  template vocabulary. A laboratory deployment requires a curated clinical
  vocabulary; coverage on arbitrary real text is unmeasured. Because unseen
  safe words quarantine conservatively, the false-quarantine metric is partly a
  vocabulary-coverage metric.
- Turkish surfaces in this corpus are written without diacritics. Diacritic and
  mixed-orthography behaviour is unmeasured.
- Results describe this synthetic corpus only. They are not a privacy,
  de-identification, clinical, or regulatory claim, and no real-data performance
  is implied.
